REDUCE YOUR EXPOSURE

Simple steps that make a real difference.

  • Use a trusted VPN or Tor to mask your IP address.
  • Try a privacy-focused browser or enable fingerprint protection.
  • Block third-party trackers and cookies.
  • Keep location, camera and microphone permissions off unless needed.
  • Keep your browser and OS updated.

Privacy: this page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent to us. The only outside request is a lookup to ipwho.is, a third-party service that turns your IP into an approximate location. IP-based location is usually city-level and often points to your provider, not your home.
